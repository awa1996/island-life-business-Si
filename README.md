# 🌴 Island Life Business AI Platform

**One App to Run Island Businesses**

Island Life Business AI Platform is a digital platform designed to help island businesses go online, manage sales, and grow using AI tools.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🏪 Business Profile Builder  
- 📦 Product & Inventory Management  
- 💳 Digital Payment Integration  
- 📢 AI Marketing Generator  
- 🤖 Island AI Business Coach  
- 🛒 Online Marketplace for Island Businesses  

---

## 🎯 Vision

To digitize every business in Solomon Islands and the Pacific Islands and create the first AI-driven island economy.

---

## 💡 Why Island Life?

Many island businesses are offline, use cash only, and have no digital tools.  
Island Life Business AI Platform helps small shops, companies, and entrepreneurs grow with technology.
